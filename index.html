Research

Research Areas

Six interconnected areas structure my research profile.

01

Sign Language Linguistics

Grammar, morphosyntax, semantics, argument structure, agreement and corpus-based research.

02

Sign Language Sociolinguistics

Variation and change, multilingual Deaf Communities, language contact, globalization and International Sign Language (IntSL).

03

Deaf Studies

Deaf-centered research, Deaf Communities, cultural knowledge, community agency and language rights.

04

Language Policy & Planning

Right to language, recognition, maintenance, transmission, planning and community-led standardization.

05

Interpreting & Translation

Interpreting, translation and mediation, professionalization, Deaf professionals, quality and multilingual practice.

06

Learning, Teaching & Assessment

CEFR, curricula, teacher professionalization, language assessment and equitable learning opportunities.

Teaching & Supervision

Research-led teaching

I teach and supervise at BA, MA and doctoral level across my research areas, with particular emphasis on early-career researchers and multilingual international research environments.

Teaching connects linguistic analysis with sociolinguistics, Deaf Studies, language policy, interpreting and translation, and sign language learning, teaching and assessment.

About

Academic Profile

I am Professor and Head of the Department of Deaf Studies and Sign Language Interpreting at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin. My academic work connects linguistic research with multilingual Deaf Communities, Deaf Studies, language rights and planning, professional interpreting and translation, and sign language education.

My work also focuses on sustainable research infrastructures, educational resources, international collaboration and community-centered research practice.
