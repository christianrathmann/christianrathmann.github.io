Research Ethics

Responsible Sign Language Research

Research ethics is an integral part of my work in Sign Language Studies and Deaf Studies. I examine participation, community expertise, informed consent, visible and identifiable sign language data, data governance, responsible reuse and emerging questions related to AI and training data.
