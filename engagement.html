Academic Engagement & Leadership

Alongside research and teaching, I contribute to academic governance, editorial work, professional standards and language-rights initiatives in Germany and internationally.

01

Academic & Scientific Leadership

Professor and Head, Department of Deaf Studies and Sign Language Interpreting, Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin (since 2017); scientific governance of the DGS Corpus Project; coordination of ProSign and DeafSign at the ECML.

02

Editorial & Scholarly Service

Editorial Board, Sign Language & Linguistics; Editorial Board, Journal of Deaf Studies and Deaf Education; Associate Editor in Frontiers language sections; Board Member, Sign Language and Linguistics Society (2014–2022).

03

Editorial Projects

Co-editor with Christopher Stone, Robert Adam and Ronice Müller de Quadros of The Routledge Handbook of Sign Language Translation and Interpreting (2022); co-editor with Richard P. Meier and Aaron Shield of the Frontiers Research Topic Modality and Language Acquisition (2023).

04

Language Rights & Community Engagement

Collaboration with Deaf organizations and European institutions on sign language recognition, individual and collective language rights, minority-language protection, accessibility and policy implementation.

05

Professional Standards, Assessment & Quality

State examination work in Hesse, national and international standardization activities in interpreting and translation, and quality assurance in accessible signed communication.

06

Academic Community Building

International summer schools, research workshops, symposia, doctoral supervision and collaborative networks connecting early-career researchers, established scholars, professionals and Deaf community experts.
