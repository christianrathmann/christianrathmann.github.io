# Christian Rathmann — Academic Research Website

Version 1.0

## Structure
- English pages are in the root directory.
- German pages are in `/de/`.
- Shared styling, images and the CV are in `/assets/`.

## Included
- Home
- Research
- Selected Publications with research-area filters
- Projects & Research Infrastructure
- Teaching & Supervision
- Academic Engagement & Leadership
- About
- Contact
- English/German navigation
- Responsive and accessible layout
- No external frameworks or tracking
