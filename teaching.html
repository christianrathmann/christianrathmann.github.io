Teaching

Teaching & Supervision

My teaching is closely connected to my research and to the development of future researchers, professionals and sign language infrastructures.

Teaching areas

Research-led teaching

01

Sign Language Linguistics

Grammar, morphosyntax, semantics, argument structure, agreement and corpus-based research.

02

Sign Language Sociolinguistics

Variation and change, multilingual Deaf Communities, language contact, globalization and International Sign Language (IntSL).

03

Deaf Studies

Deaf-centered perspectives on Deaf Communities, cultural knowledge, community agency, institutional power and language rights.

04

Language Policy & Planning

The right to language, transmission, recognition, maintenance, language planning and community-led standardization.

05

Interpreting & Translation

Interpreting, translation and mediation, professionalization, multilingual practice, Deaf professionals and quality.

06

Sign Language Learning, Teaching & Assessment

CEFR-based approaches, curricula, teacher professionalization, language assessment and equitable learning opportunities.

Supervision

Supervision

I supervise BA, MA and doctoral research that connects linguistic analysis with sociolinguistic, educational, cultural and institutional questions.

  • sign language grammar and structure
  • sociolinguistic variation and change
  • multilingual Deaf Communities
  • International Sign Language (IntSL)
  • Deaf Studies
  • language policy and planning
  • interpreting, translation and mediation
  • Deaf interpreters, translators and mediators
  • sign language learning and teaching
  • language assessment
  • sign language resources and research infrastructures

Capacity Building

Early-Career Research & Capacity Building

International summer schools, workshops, research seminars and collaborative projects create spaces for methodological training, peer exchange, research presentation and interdisciplinary discussion.

My approach emphasizes academic independence, methodological rigor, critical reflection and sustainable research networks.
