Teaching & Supervision

Teaching & Supervision

My teaching and supervision connect Sign Language Studies, Deaf Studies and Interpreting Studies with linguistic, sociolinguistic, educational and professional perspectives.

Teaching

Research-Based Learning

I teach at BA, MA and doctoral level and connect theory, empirical research and professional practice. My teaching includes sign language linguistics and sociolinguistics, Deaf Studies, language policy and planning, interpreting and translation, sign language acquisition and learning, language assessment and research methods.

Professional Education

Interpreting & Translation

In interpreting and translation education, I connect linguistic knowledge with interpreting and translation processes, professional competence, ethics, quality and institutional practice. My own experience as a certified interpreter and translator informs this connection between academic research, professional education and practice.

Supervision

BA, MA & Doctoral Research

I supervise research across my six research areas. I encourage students and emerging researchers to develop theoretically informed questions, work critically with empirical evidence and situate their research within relevant linguistic, social and institutional contexts.

Early Career Researchers

Capacity Building

A particular focus of my academic work is supporting early-career researchers and creating multilingual and internationally connected research environments in which emerging researchers can develop their own academic profiles and participate actively in research communities.

Research Ethics

Responsible Research

Research ethics is integrated into my teaching and supervision, with attention to informed consent, participation, community expertise, visual sign language data, data governance, responsible research practices and emerging technologies.

International Learning

Multilingual Perspectives

Courses, supervision and research activities bring together students and researchers working with different sign languages, written languages and academic traditions, encouraging comparative and multilingual perspectives.

Connection

Teaching, Supervision & Research

Teaching, supervision and research are closely connected in my academic work: research questions inform teaching, while teaching and supervision continually generate new perspectives for research.
