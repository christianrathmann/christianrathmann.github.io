Christian Rathmann

About

Professor for Sign Language Interpreting, linguist and certified interpreter and translator.

I am Professor for Sign Language Interpreting at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin, a linguist and a certified interpreter and translator. My academic work is situated at the intersection of Sign Language Studies, Deaf Studies and Interpreting Studies.

My research examines how sign languages are structured, used, transmitted, learned, taught and mediated across multilingual Deaf communities and institutional contexts. I am particularly interested in the relationships between linguistic structure, multilingualism, language rights, education, professional communication and institutional practice.

A recurring focus of my work is the connection between academic research and sustainable infrastructures, including corpus research, digital language resources, language assessment, educational development and international capacity building. I also work on research ethics, accessibility and the implications of AI and sign language technologies for Deaf communities.

My professional experience as an interpreter and translator complements my academic work and informs my interest in interpreting and translation processes, professional competence, quality and institutional responsibility.

Academic background: PhD in Linguistics, The University of Texas at Austin. Academic positions in Germany, the United Kingdom and the United States. Professor and Head at Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin since 2017.

Professional qualification: State-certified interpreter and translator since 2011. Working languages include German Sign Language (DGS), International Sign Language (IntSL), American Sign Language (ASL), German and English.
