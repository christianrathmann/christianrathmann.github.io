Research, Infrastructure & Collaboration

My academic work connects empirical research with sustainable infrastructures, capacity building, educational development and international collaboration.

Research infrastructure

DGS Corpus Project & Language Resources

Long-term corpus research and infrastructure for German Sign Language (DGS), together with SignBank 2.0 and VisuoLab, support linguistic and sociolinguistic research, language documentation and applications in education and interpreting.

ECML · Council of Europe

DeafSign

European collaboration on equitable and sustainable sign language learning opportunities, with a focus on multilingualism, educational access, learner diversity and teacher competences.

DeafSign at ECML
CEFR & sign languages

ProSign

ProSign connects sign language learning, teaching and assessment with the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR), including proficiency descriptors, curriculum development, teacher competences and assessment.

ProSign at ECML
Interpreting & translation

DMIT

DMIT examines professional roles and practices of Deaf mediators, interpreters and translators, including education, recognition, employment, collaboration and Deaf-led professionalization.

Related research area
Tallinn University

EVKUR

EVKUR connects research on Estonian Sign Language with long-term capacity building in language structure and use, sociolinguistics, resources, learning and teaching, assessment and language policy.

EVKUR at Tallinn University

STEMSiL

International collaboration on STEM learning through sign languages, connecting subject-specific concepts, educational resources and accessible learning opportunities for Deaf children.

Free Technology Signs

Language literacy

DGS Storybooks for Deaf Children

In close collaboration with Chris Peters, I work on storybooks for Deaf children in German Sign Language (DGS), connecting early language access, storytelling, literacy and cultural transmission. RISE Videobooks provides an important international context for shared reading in sign languages.
