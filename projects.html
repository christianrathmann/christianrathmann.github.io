Projects & Research Infrastructure

My research is closely connected to sustainable research infrastructures, educational resources, professional networks and institutional structures for sign languages and Deaf Communities.

German Sign Language Corpus · Research Infrastructure

DGS Corpus Project

From 2009 to 2017, I led the DGS Corpus Project. Since 2018, I have continued to contribute to its scientific governance as Chair of the Scientific Committee of the DGS Corpus Project at the Academy of Sciences and Humanities in Hamburg. The corpus supports empirical research on DGS structure, use, variation and change.

DeafSign

DeafSign – Unlocking Educational Opportunities in Sign Languages in Europe – examines how equitable and sustainable sign language learning opportunities can be strengthened across Europe. The project addresses multilingualism, educational access, teacher competences, learning resources and Deaf learners with refugee and immigration backgrounds. I coordinate DeafSign at the ECML.

Deaf Mediators, Interpreters and Translators

DMIT

DMIT investigates professional roles, education, recognition and employment of Deaf mediators, interpreters and translators. The research examines differentiated professional profiles, training and assessment, access to assignments, collaboration, institutional gatekeeping and Deaf-led professional governance.

Estonian Sign Language Research Centre · Tallinn University

EVKUR

EVKUR develops sustainable research, teaching and language-resource infrastructures for Estonian Sign Language (EVK). Its programme connects sign language structure and use with sociolinguistics, language planning, teaching, learning, assessment and digital resources.

ProSign

ProSign developed European reference resources for sign language teaching, learning and assessment within the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR). I was a project member from 2011 to 2016 and project coordinator from 2016 to 2019.

Fachstelle Teilhabeberatung

Since 2017, my work with the BMAS-funded Fachstelle Teilhabeberatung has connected academic expertise in sign languages, Deaf Studies and translation with accessible communication and institutional practice. I lead the Focus Team Accessibility and contribute to quality assurance, translation and review processes, accessible event formats and practical recommendations.
